SlushAI — generate a 3D mesh in your browser

TripoSR image-to-3D, running entirely on your device with ONNX Runtime Web. No server, no upload, no account. The model weights (~690 MB) download once and are cached by your browser. Generation takes about 2–4 minutes on a desktop CPU.

Draft quality: TripoSR is a working generator with a real back (a closed mesh),
not the immaculate one — topology can be chaotic and details blurred. The upgrade path is
SF3D, then TripoSG. Background removal is not in this page yet: use an image with a clean
background (a transparent PNG works best; its stored RGB is used, alpha ignored). Drag the
preview to rotate. The mesh is yours to keep — feed the GLB back into SlushAI for remesh
and PBR texturing.
